How information submitted through this website is intended to be handled.

1. Information you provide

When you contact Rhythm ∞ Raatri or submit an enquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, event details, location, preferences and any additional information you choose to share.

Information submitted through this website is intended to help us respond to enquiries, understand event requirements, communicate about a requested service and maintain relevant business records.

Information should be retained only for as long as required for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal and business record obligations. This policy may be updated when website functionality or business practices change. The current version should be published on this page.
